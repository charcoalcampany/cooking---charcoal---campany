About Our Company

Our project and purpose

Cooking Charcoal Company is a student project from GGSS Kura - January 2026.

Our project explores useful cooking fuel made from available agricultural materials and biomass waste such as rice husk.

We aim to promote practical energy solutions and better use of agricultural waste by converting suitable materials into useful charcoal products.

Production

From agricultural waste to useful charcoal

Our project explores the conversion of agricultural waste such as rice husk into useful charcoal products.

Our goal is to encourage innovation, responsible use of available materials and useful cooking fuel solutions.
