About Our Company

Cooking Charcoal Company is a student project from GGSS Kura - January 2026.

Our project focuses on exploring useful cooking fuel from available agricultural materials, including biomass waste such as rice husk.

Our goal is to explore practical energy solutions for cooking while making better use of suitable agricultural waste materials.

🎯

Our Mission

To explore useful cooking fuel solutions and encourage creative use of suitable agricultural biomass materials.

👁️
